01 / Problem

Why the platform was needed

The challenge was not simply ticketing. IT administration involved many recurring records and checks that were spread across separate workflows and required manual follow-up.

01

Fragmented visibility

Assets, domains, SSL status, endpoint information, server health, subscriptions, and operational records needed a more centralized view.

02

Recurring manual checks

Website, SSL, backup, endpoint, and infrastructure checks created repetitive monthly and daily administrative work.

03

Operational traceability

IT work needed clearer status tracking, historical records, access controls, warnings, and repeatable reporting.

04 / Engineering Decisions

How I approached the work

These decisions reflect the operational goals documented in the portfolio and résumé, while omitting private implementation details.

Challenge

Too much recurring manual inspection

Website, SSL, backup, endpoint and infrastructure checks created repetitive administrative work.

Approach

Use Bash-based and scheduled automation where appropriate, then surface results through centralized operational workflows and records.

Challenge

Multiple operational data types

Domains, endpoints, servers, subscriptions, access records and tickets have different lifecycles and status requirements.

Approach

Keep each operational area distinct while presenting it through a consistent IT administration platform and access model.

Challenge

Production security and privacy

Administrative systems contain sensitive operational information that should not be exposed publicly.

Approach

Use role-based access controls internally and publish only sanitized screenshots, descriptions and conceptual architecture in this portfolio.
